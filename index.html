COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful information

How do I request an appointment?

Use “Request an Appointment” to see the available options, or call the office. An appointment is confirmed only after the office confirms it.

Which insurance plans are accepted?

Please call the office and your insurer to confirm participation and coverage before your visit.

Can I send medical information through this website?

This public website does not collect medical information. Use the practice’s approved patient portal when connected, or call the office for instructions.

What if I need urgent help?

Do not use this website, email, or portal for emergencies. For a medical emergency, call 911.
